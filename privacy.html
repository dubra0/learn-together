Talk Together

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use · Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Privacy Policy

Talk Together ("the app") lets people practice languages in live voice rooms. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account: a random account ID created when you start, and the display name you choose.
  • Profile (optional): profile photo, country, languages, level and a short bio that you add yourself.
  • Activity: rooms you create, people you follow or block, words you save, and practice statistics (minutes, points, streak).
  • Reports: if you report someone, we store the report and the reason you give.

Voice and chat

  • Voice in rooms is transmitted live and is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Chat messages in rooms are sent live to people in the room and are not stored on our servers.
  • Everything you say or write in a room can be heard or seen by everyone in that room.

Services we use

  • Supabase — account, profiles and room list storage.
  • LiveKit — real-time voice delivery.
  • Google Translate — only if you tap "Translate"; the text opens in Google Translate.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads based on your data.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and older. If we learn that a user is under 13, the account will be removed.

Deleting your account

In the app open Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photo, rooms, follows and saved words. You can also ask us to delete your data by email (see Contact).

2. Terms of Use

  • You must be 13 years or older to use the app.
  • Be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats, spam and sharing other people's private information are not allowed.
  • Hosts can move people to listeners or remove them from a room. Moderators can block accounts that break these rules.
  • Use Report on a profile or room to tell us about a problem. Use Block to stop hearing and seeing someone.
  • The app is provided "as is" without guarantees. We may change or stop features at any time.
